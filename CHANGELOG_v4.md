# Changelog v4

- Focused the build on Level 1 only.
- Placed the current spider-mech boss in Level 1.
- Added persistent fog-of-war exploration and an explored-only minimap.
- Imported eight-direction idle and walking animation frames from the Pygame v10 asset pack.
- Replaced the micro-grid floor with four larger worn-floor variants.
- Spread weapon pickups across Level 1 and require defeating the boss plus drones before exiting.

No Level 2 or Level 3 progression is active in this version.
