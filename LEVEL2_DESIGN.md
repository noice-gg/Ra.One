# Ra.One — Level 2: The Null Foundry

## Purpose
Level 2 raises the threat after the Spider-Mech fight without changing the established movement, weapons, minimap, fog-of-war, data pickups, upgrade terminal, or combat controls.

## Visual identity
- Industrial foundry floor with dark steel, coolant pools, violet slag fields, and red warning light.
- Three landmarks: Coolant Exchange, Sentinel Foundry, and Overlord Approach.
- Denser cover and a deliberate route through three combat pockets.
- Generated, project-specific pixel-art character concepts converted into transparent PNG enemy sprites.

## Enemies
- **Foundry Sentinel:** slower, tougher unit with a ranged attack.
- **Foundry Striker:** faster unit that pressures the player at closer range and deals heavier contact damage.
- **Chaos Overlord:** Level 2 boss with a larger health pool, faster projectiles, a wider radial burst, and a dedicated boss-music phase.

Enemy positions are randomized from walkable cells on each run; the boss appears only after the level's regular enemies are defeated. The exit gate is hidden until the boss falls.

## Progression
- Level 1 victory presents **Continue to Level 2**.
- Level 2 keeps the player's remaining health, collected data, weapon ownership, and weapon upgrades.
- Level 2 has a fresh set of data fragments and weapon caches to reward exploration.
- The final gate leads to a Level 2 victory screen. The Engine Core is reserved as the next chapter rather than falsely presenting the campaign as complete.

## Controls
WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, left-click to fire, Shift + left-click for Rail Blaster, right-click for Nova Core, Space to dash, E to upgrade, 1–4 to switch weapon slots, Tab for the CGG algorithm panel, Esc to pause.
