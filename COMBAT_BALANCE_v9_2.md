# v9.2 Combat Balance Update

## Health
- G.One: 100 HP in Level 1; 200 HP on transition to Level 2.
- Security Drone: 20 HP.
- Spider-Mech boss: 250 HP.
- Sentinel: 35 HP.
- Striker: 25 HP.
- Chaos Overlord summoned Sentinel: 30 HP.
- Chaos Overlord boss: 500 HP.
- Added visible world medkits in both levels. Collecting one restores 20 HP, capped at maximum health. Medkits remain available while the player is at full health.

## Player weapon damage
All weapons now have three upgrade levels:
- Pulse Gun: 3 / 6 / 9 damage per projectile.
- Laser Blade: 4 / 8 / 12 damage per activation hit.
- Rail Blaster standard projectile: 5 / 10 / 15.
- Rail Blaster beam: 6 / 12 / 18 per enemy hit.
- Nova Core: 10 / 20 / 30 per enemy inside its blast radius.

## Enemy damage
All enemy attack damage is a multiple of 5:
- Level 1 drone: 5 projectile damage, 10 contact damage.
- Level 1 Spider-Mech: 15 regular projectile, 10 radial projectile, 20 aimed projectile/contact damage.
- Level 2 Sentinel: 10 projectile/contact damage.
- Level 2 Striker: 15 projectile/contact damage.
- Level 2 Chaos Overlord: 20 regular projectile, 15 radial projectile, 25 aimed projectile/contact damage.
- Summoned Sentinels use Level 2 Sentinel damage.
